`j-toastie-rigged-astronaut.glb` is the original downloaded asset. It is used as the player astronaut and posed at runtime through its Mixamo skeleton. The downloaded GLB includes no canned animation clips.

`j-toastie-rigged-astronaut-no-pack.glb` is a local derivative generated by
`scripts/refine-astronaut.mjs`. It removes the disconnected backpack shell and
two rear fittings while retaining the original mesh vertices, skin weights,
skeleton, materials, helmet, and closed suit torso. The original download is
kept alongside the derivative.

The derivative is distributed under CC BY 3.0 with the attribution above. No endorsement by J-Toastie is implied.

Forest Crew packages only the no-pack derivative; the original source model and modification script remain in the independently maintained Hand Walk project.

Forest Crew further adjusts the suit material colors and smooths mesh normals at runtime; the skeleton and skin weights remain intact.
